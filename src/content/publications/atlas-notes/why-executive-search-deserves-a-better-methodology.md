## The problem with the standard model

*Placeholder body content.* Executive Search has changed remarkably little in
thirty years. The mandate is issued, a longlist is produced, a shortlist follows,
and a decision is made — largely on impressions formed in a handful of
conversations.

## A decision discipline instead

Placeholder paragraph. When a search is treated as a decision, the sequence
changes: the market is established first, the criteria are agreed second, and
candidates are evaluated against evidence that can be reviewed months later.

### What that requires

1. A structured discovery conversation before any approach is made.
2. A written blueprint of the role, the market and the decision criteria.
3. A consistent evaluation lens applied to every candidate.

> Placeholder pull quote. The quality of a hire is a function of the quality of
> the decision that produced it.
