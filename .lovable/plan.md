# Verify the new publication page

The article page for the test Atlas Note already responds successfully at
`/knowledge-hub/why-executive-search-deserves-a-better-methodology`. What is
left is a visual check that it looks right, and small fixes if it doesn't.

## Steps

1. Open the Knowledge Hub and confirm the card for "Why Executive Search
   deserves a better methodology." is clickable and leads to the article page.
2. Open the article page and check, top to bottom: category label, title,
   excerpt, date, reading time, author, key findings, figures, body text with
   its headings, lists and quote, sources, related items, and the closing
   call to action.
3. Confirm the reading column is narrower than the rest of the site, the
   spacing is generous, and headings, tables and figures match the existing
   Sync Talent look.
4. Check the page on a narrow phone-width screen.
5. Fix anything that looks off — spacing, sizes, a section rendering when it
   should stay hidden, or a broken link.

## Notes

- No new pages, no redesign, no changes to the salary numbers or existing
  titles.
- Only the shared article template and its styles would be touched if a fix
  is needed.
